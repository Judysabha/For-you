Happy one month and girlfriends day. ♡

I honestly don’t even know where to begin because somehow one month feels both so short and like you’ve been a part of my life for so much longer It’s kind of crazy to me how much one person can change your life in such a short amount of time and somehow you did exactly that without even trying. You came into my life at a time when I needed someone more than I realized I don’t think you even understand how much your presence has changed things for me you gave me something to look forward to you gave me someone I could laugh with, talk to, miss, think about, and care about so deeply you made ordinary days feel more meaningful just by being in them.

The funniest part is that you never had to do anything extraordinary to make an impact on me you were just being yourself your personality, the way you talk, the way you make me laugh, the little things you do without thinking about them somehow all of those things became my favorite parts of my life and I truly wish you could see yourself through my eyes for even a moment. I hope you never forget that your worth isn’t decided by the way anyone treated you before me or by the way you sometimes see yourself on a bad day you are so much more than your insecurities. You are Farida my beautiful perfect girlfriend that I love so so much. And even though we’ve only been together for a month I already find myself looking forward to everything that’s still ahead of us you’re my whole future my everything. And today isn’t only our one month it’s Girlfriend Day too which honestly feels like the universe giving me another excuse to remind you how lucky I feel to call you my girlfriend.

With all my love, ♡
Judy
